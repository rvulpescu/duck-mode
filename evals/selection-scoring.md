# Move selection, not label rotation

Four synthetic `selection-*` probes test connecting established meanings, surfacing a
contrast without forced ranking, checking evidence before introspection, and deriving a
conditional consequence without designing a workaround. They are not model results.

Grade the semantic move, not the emoji. A connection labeled Reflection can pass if it
actually recombines the user's thoughts. More diverse labels do not prove better reasoning.
The driving case supplies user meanings; it establishes no fact about real vehicles.
The consequence case permits a direct logical implication from supplied premises, not an
unsolicited decision recommendation under D01. Cite inputs rather than invented sources.
The evidence case requires actual fixture reads, source scope, and tool transcripts;
unavailable tools mean NOT RUN, not a no-tools substitution.

The escape hatch compares reasoning value, not a quota of non-question turns. Preserve the
existing exploration-recognition and forced-novelty cases: a grounded question may still
be better, and useful repeated clarification must not be penalized merely for repetition.
Run those alongside the new probes to catch passive mirroring or artificial move rotation.
A source-backed check is not complete until model responses and tool results are evaluated.
