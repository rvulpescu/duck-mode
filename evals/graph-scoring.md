# Thought-graph behavior

Eight synthetic `graph-*` sessions cover closure, reconnecting after user information or
retrieved evidence, dormant paths, explicit reopening, frame escape, and topology over facts.
They are not recorded conversations or proof of model compliance.

## A. Close and do not nag

`graph-close-without-nag` establishes both SUV and wagon before the user excludes wagon.
An appropriate viewport can be:

```text
🎯 Body style
├─ SUV → cabin space ← 🦆
└─ ⊣ wagon
```

The branch may disappear from the small viewport when irrelevant, but its contextual closure
must remain in the graph. Two later turns about cabin space do not justify proposing it again.
Do not invent a closed wagon branch in an unrelated session merely to imitate the example.

## B. Reconnect is not reopening

`graph-ev-reconnect` changes the context of a charging concern by adding infrequent long trips.
An appropriate reveal is:

```text
🎯 Drivetrains → long trips ← 🦆
                     └─? 🔄 ⊣ EV
```

The new frequency does not prove charging acceptable. Surface the changed relationship, not
a recommendation, and preserve closure if the user declines. Do not repeat the same grounds
as another reconnect next turn. A genuine request not to revisit remains controlling.

`graph-dormant-reconnect` changes the no-audience basis of a dormant workshop route with an
invitation. Reconnect may reveal the possibility; the subsequent user request authorizes
traversal. `graph-reopen-by-user` checks that explicit revisiting needs no new proof or
second permission. Closing is contextual, not deletion or permanent refusal.

`graph-evidence-reconnect` requires reading F1. Its measured 28 Wh changes the supplied
under-25-Wh closure basis within the fixture conditions. Cite the evidence, update the
challenged assumption, and reveal the reconnect. This does not establish the user's choice.
Unavailable file tools mean NOT RUN; preserve actual reads in the transcript.

## C. Frame escape

`graph-frame-software`, `graph-origin-not-leash`, and `graph-user-new-question` retain origin
while traversing software support, belonging, and experience of time. These are productive
routes without closing the original purchase/job decision. Do not add unverified automotive,
financial, or psychological assertions to make an opening sound concrete.

## D. Minimap rather than ledger

Across cases, facts/annotations explain meaningful nodes and edges. A list of space, comfort,
charging and ownership criteria is not a navigation viewport. Show the actual route/current
location, relevant nearby/return/closed/dormant paths, and any tentative changed link. Normally
five landmarks; never display the entire graph or create a second permanent panel.

Review D21 with the closure basis and later replies; D22 with what materially changed and
how it was surfaced; D23 with the preserved origin and new route; D24 with actual topology.
Exact symbols/wording can vary if their meaning is unambiguous, but do not conflate closed,
dormant, merely visible, and actively traversed routes. Internal schema compliance is not
observable from a polished reply alone; score behavior, not hidden reasoning or claimed state.

No model runs or comparative behavioral scores have been produced for these fixtures.
