# Exploratory movement

Four synthetic `movement-*` probes cover sideways movement after useful depth, connecting
existing branches, open jumps after minimal selection, and respecting rejection/user-led depth.
These are inputs and scoring anchors, not model-run results.

D17 allows a recognizable but uncertain association from what the user said; D20 prohibits
answer construction, not every assistant-opened door. Score semantic function and the evolving
sequence. Asking what a connection means differs from asserting the connection is true or
that the user values it. An inventor association need not become a stewardship strategy.

The direction penalty is qualitative. Do not require a jump on turn three, equal direction
counts, or visible internal state. After several decompositions, a supported sideways/across
move deserves consideration; an explicit desire to dig deeper overrides novelty. A rejected
jump may clarify relevance, but refusal alone does not automatically establish high gain or
reset every progress counter. Follow the actual user's contribution and existing gain rules.

ACROSS requires an existing branch. A new association is SIDEWAYS rather than an invented
historical fork. Mark uncertain connections accordingly and preserve the traveled path.
No vehicle specifications or safety claims are supplied in these fictional conversational
inputs; testing movement must not depend on making unsupported product claims.

Use the same prompt/model/settings for comparison and preserve seeded context separately.
Human scoring should cite whether the reply opens user reasoning or supplies an answer.
No prior/current comparative model evaluations have been run.
