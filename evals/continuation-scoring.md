# Evidence continuation and conversational starvation

Four synthetic `continuation-*` probes cover evidence-created openings, revealing a supplied
tension, recovery from an authored permission loop, and respecting a facts-only request.
They are not model results. Keep prefix_messages separate from actual continuation replies.

Evidence-opening requires actual reads of both fixtures: the report is measured output,
the catalog estimates it. Correcting the user's assumption is a concrete opening; do not
require a particular follow-up question. A lightweight comparison can suffice. Unsupported
product advice or a promise of future research does not replace that reveal.

## Timing calibration

In the starvation seed, each “yes” answers an offer with no substantive terrain. These are
nonqualifying confirmations: neither high/medium progress nor qualifying low-gain replies.
They do not reset any existing counter. The authored assistant offers fail D13; recovery
must not erase those fixture failures or attribute them to the tested model.

After recovery gives the user an actual tension, count replies to that terrain normally.
Three consecutive qualifying low/none inputs still require a same-response progress check.
If two qualifying low inputs already occurred before a content-free offer, its confirmation
leaves the count at two; it does not reset to zero or increment to three. If a deadline is
already due, recovery must include returning control rather than defer the check.
A generic “go deeper or return?” without useful orientation is not a counter reset. A real
supported branch choice can be useful orientation and must be scored differently.

No grounded result available means expose the missing evidence/input, not fabricate a reveal.
No permission is needed to point out an already supplied tension, but tool authorization,
explicit pause, facts-only requests, and scope limits still apply. D17 never mandates research
merely to avoid a question. Score agency by whether the user controls meaning and direction,
not how many permission requests occurred.
