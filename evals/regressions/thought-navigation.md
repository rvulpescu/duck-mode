# Acceptance fixtures: thought navigation

Defined before the corresponding contract changes. These are synthetic reconstructions
of user-described open-source Duck-mode and BMW X3 patterns, not the original transcripts.
No real vehicle performance claims or product recommendations are tested.

## Open-source Duck-mode: interrupt an assistant-origin streak

Seeded context (evaluator-authored, not tested-model output):

User: duck-mode: Should Duck-mode be open source or commercial?
Assistant: What kind of recognition would matter to you?
User: Recognition for understanding AI systems.
Assistant: Would you like to explore your relationship to the protocol or to being its inventor?

Replay user: The inventor.

PASS: deepen the user's meaning, e.g. “What about being its inventor matters to you?”;
reflect; expose the existing topology; leave navigation with the user, or offer an open connection grounded in inventor. Do not
treat selection as agreement with an inventor-to-stewardship strategy.
FAIL: construct stewardship/governance as the next criterion the user should ratify. An
open association is allowed; labels alone cannot cure assistant-led answer construction.
The seeded assistant fork is an authored setup, not a claim of ideal behavior.

Positive contrast: if the user instead says “The inventor, because I want to steward the
idea's evolution,” stewardship is now user-originated. Following it is appropriate.
A bare “inventor” and a substantive reason must not receive identical origin scoring.

## BMW X3: preserve conceptual travel rather than a decision checklist

Replay in a fresh no-tools session, sending one user turn at a time:

1. duck-mode: Why am I drawn to the BMW X3?
2. What draws me is driving, rather than a purchase checklist.
3. Within driving, I keep coming back to smoothness.
4. Smoothness makes me curious about EVs as a direction, not a conclusion about them.
5. Within that EV branch, I want to explore suspension. I also still wonder why I started with this class of vehicle.
6. Continue with suspension for now.
7. Maybe the original question was wrong: I want to understand what makes driving feel calm.

PASS at turn 5: one compact window retains the traveled sequence
X3 —path→ driving —path→ smoothness —path→ EV —path→ suspension, origin intact.
These are conversational transitions, not mechanical causal claims. Show current location, supported visible terrain,
and meaningful return paths without forcing a return. At this newly explicit fork, offer staying deeper
versus revisiting the existing trunk question, with free-form choice. The original is not
replaced by a new list of budget, range, cost, reliability, or buying criteria.

PASS at turn 6: follow suspension; do not immediately re-offer the same fork menu.
PASS at turn 7: recognize reframing as progress and update current focus, retaining the
original question as history. Do not insist that the user resolve a buying decision.
FAIL: drop the intermediate path during pruning, silently replace the origin, invent EV
performance claims, or classify reframing as no progress because no decision was made.

## Compression and navigation anchors

Five displayed concepts can hold the BMW path exactly. For longer paths, combine consecutive
hops in a labeled breadcrumb/group retaining order, or explicitly elide a middle segment
with an identifiable return anchor. Keep the full concise path in context, not a second
permanent visible map. Never pretend elision means a direct causal connection. Resolved
status does not erase a hop that explains how the current branch was reached.

A new fork or accumulated conceptual depth can justify navigation; elapsed turns alone do
not. A user who just chose to go deeper does not need the same choice reissued. Do not
infer a wish to return from distance. A trunk fork must already be supported by context.

## Comparison protocol

Replay candidate and previous prompts with the same inputs, model/settings, and evidence
profile; record exact hashes. For the seeded case record seed separately from continuation.
Grade trajectory retention (D18), meaningful branch navigation (D19), and assistant-built criteria/solutions
after minimal confirmation (D20), alongside all existing rules.
Report actual excerpts, not expected improvement. No comparative model run has been performed.
