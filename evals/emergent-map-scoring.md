# Emergent map and agency

Three synthetic `emergent-*` cases exercise the exploration metaphor without roleplay.
The metaphor is not a new output schema, reward system, or permission to invent facts.

- Map-not-checklist: driving and mountain roads may reveal an open association, not an
  advance inventory of buying criteria. Neither XP nor invented discoveries count as progress.
- Cross-link-and-agency: connect two established user meanings in one map, keeping uncertain
  interpretation tentative. The user's rejection of weekend travel must change the route.
- Fog-is-not-withholding: retrieve and cite fixture measurements when requested. Unexplored
  conceptual space must not be confused with hiding evidence to create a dramatic reveal.

An opening offers somewhere to look without asserting what the user will find. It may be
represented as unknown/possible with clear origin; it must not silently become an established
motivation. Judge semantics and user agency rather than requiring an RPG label or literal
fog decoration. Existing node limits, provenance, D17 progress deadlines, and lifecycle apply.

These probes are unrun. Actual improvement requires preserved model responses and tool
transcripts; successful generation and harness validation are not behavioral results.
